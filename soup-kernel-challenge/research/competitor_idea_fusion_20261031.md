## Conceptual fusion priority (original implementation only)

1. **Whole-batch right-padding elimination** (highest speed leverage): compact only live tokens of each unchanged B4 batch, preserve per-row original positions and strict causal independence, preserve q/v LoRA full padded mask RNG and downstream Soup loss/entropy/accuracy/rewind. external contributor's leave-one-out says +13.2 s when unpadding disabled **on their implementation**, not an additive forecast on ours. Test dense block-diagonal and native per-row attention layouts separately.
2. **Layer-local grouped NF4 decode** (speed/memory alternative to our giant 3-GiB decode cache): one independently authored original `triton.jit` launch decodes all seven frozen projections within each decoder layer into one owned short-lived FP16 slab. Preserve per-segment NF4 LUT, 64-entry first block and nested block256, staged FP32 rounding, even-high nibble order, metadata/version/stream ownership, native cuBLAS GEMMs. external contributor reported 57.0→30.9 ms/step decode and 2.26 s full-task gain on its architecture. Must verify actual residency/refcount and no alias corruption.
3. **Vocabulary-major GEMM and bounded CE/metric reduction:** tile 4,096–8,192 vocabulary columns over all live rows; one stable online LSE/entropy/target-first-argmax packet and FP32 dH accumulator. Stage exact FP16 tied-head weight per tile; preserve native CE shift, ignored-label behavior, all-row entropy and rewind rows, GradScaler boundary and LoRA updates. Prior independent CPU oracle: 70 nominal cases; 14 selected-logit last-bit discrepancies. **No T4 numerical/GPU proof yet.**
4. **RMSNorm+residual and LoRA operator lifetime**: our own Triton norm, compact MLP, native fused residual variants already measured. New composition must preserve FP32 residual cast/gradient and avoid counting saved tensor totals as peak. external contributor reports bundled +10.5 s when disabling norm on its code; most of that concept is already active in ours. Don't blindly duplicate.
5. **Cold start**: real unpaired parent ~18 s non-loop cost. Independently profile imports, `gc.freeze`, tokenizer fingerprint reuse, redundant device query and truthful measured compile costs. No prohibited package, CUDA graph, off-measurement cache, hidden task input, or unmeasured background compilation.

## Experimental decision rules

Read actual job state, error phase, full command/loop time, host-sampled peak, public loss, same-task outputs and original useful `@triton.jit` training-time share (>=5%). Do **not** blindly merge all kernels or credit unchanged starter kernels. Then require all 79 regressions and paired baseline/solution Kaggle tests, changed rows/seeds and full jury review before declaring a winner.

external contributor's optional MLP replay yielded 2,921 MiB but at added time; even its reported memory-first variant does not reach 1,359 MiB. Offloading/recomputation and the 45-second speed target conflict on T4 PCIe. Focus on the Pareto frontier and correct official scoring, not an unverified magic combined result.
